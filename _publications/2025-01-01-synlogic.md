**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

**GitHub:** [hkust-nlp/SynLogic](https://github.com/hkust-nlp/SynLogic)

**Abstract:** We introduce SynLogic, a scalable framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale, enabling learning of logical reasoning and beyond. Our approach generates high-quality, self-consistent reasoning chains for complex logical problems, leveraging verifiable programs and automated correctness checks. The resulting dataset serves as strong training signals for large language models, significantly improving their logical reasoning capabilities across multiple benchmarks.

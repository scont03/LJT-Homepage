**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We propose In-Context Sharpness as a signal for hallucination detection, viewing the LLM's inner representations from a geometric perspective. When the model generates uncertain or hallucinated outputs, the sharpness of the output distribution — measured locally in representation space — differs significantly from that of truthful, confident generations. Our framework shows that in-context representations of high-sharpness outputs correlate with hallucinations, enabling interpretable, plug-and-play alerting mechanisms across model families.

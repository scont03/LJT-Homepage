**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**GitHub:** [Vision4Chart/Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vision4Chart/Vision4Chart)

**Abstract:** We identify and analyze a fundamental perception bottleneck in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when processing visual charts. By designing targeted evaluation benchmarks and intervention studies, we demonstrate that VLMs often fail to accurately parse and extract numerical information from visual chart elements, even when the charts contain only basic, well-structured visual information. Our analysis points to limitations in how VLMs process spatial layout, axis labeling, and bar/line value encoding, with implications for chart understanding tasks across domains.

**Authors:** Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level, multi-discipline evaluation suite for Chinese foundation models. It covers 52 subjects across humanities, social sciences, STEM, and professional exams, with varying difficulty levels from middle school to doctoral. C-Eval provides a standardized benchmark for assessing Chinese-language model capabilities, revealing strengths and weaknesses across domains and supporting model development.

**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

**GitHub:** [hkust-nlp/Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane](https://github.com/hkust-nlp/Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane)

**Abstract:** We identify a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside the residual representations of LLMs that distinguishes truthful from untruthful statements across diverse model families and sizes. This hyperplane emerges as a consistent linear separator in intermediate transformer layers, correlates with truthfulness judgments, and can be leveraged for truthfulness detection and intervention. Our findings offer new insights into how LLMs encode factual accuracy internally and provide practical tools for detecting hallucinations.

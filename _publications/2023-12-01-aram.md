**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We demonstrate that parameter-efficient modules (PEMs) such as LoRA adapters can be effectively composed using arithmetic operations — addition, subtraction, and interpolation — to produce new models that generalize to unseen tasks and transfer settings. Our approach enables modular arithmetic on adapted models without retraining, with applications to model merging, task vector composition, and knowledge editing. We provide empirical evidence that arithmetic on PEM vectors corresponds to meaningful transformations in the model's functional space.

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), supervised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://junxianh.github.io/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** - Designing scalable data synthesis and training frameworks to teach LLMs complex reasoning capabilities
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** - Understanding and mitigating perception bottlenecks in multimodal models
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** - Discovering universal representations of truthfulness and developing early detection mechanisms
